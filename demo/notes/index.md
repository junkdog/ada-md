## Habits

She takes her **first nap** _after breakfast_, in the sun by the south window, and the second
on whoever sits down first. Evenings are for patrolling the hallway and inspecting the
groceries.

### Sleeping spots

1. the south window, mornings
2. the rug in the hallway, afternoons[^rug]
3. the warm side of the laptop, whenever it's open

### Words she knows

`treats`, `brush`, `vet` (she leaves the room), and her name, which she ignores.

[^rug]: The rug is hers. It was bought for the hallway, and she agreed to it.
